CARDOWAY SUPPORT

How can we help?

Cardoway supports iPhone models running iOS 17 or later.

How do I restore my full-version purchase?

Open Settings in Cardoway, find the full-version panel, and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase and can connect to the App Store.

What happens when I close the app?

A fresh game starts after you fully close and reopen the app; unfinished games are not saved. Your settings and recorded stats stay. Buying or restoring the extra themes requires an internet connection.

What is the difference between the two play modes?

Choose how to play • Relaxed: matching cards are collected automatically. • Observation: find and tap an earlier card of the same rank yourself. A wrong choice or a pass ends your turn.

Why is a theme locked?

The game includes both modes and one table theme. Four more themes and their matching effects are available through a single, optional purchase. You can preview them first. There is no subscription.
